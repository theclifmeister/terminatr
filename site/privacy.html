Privacy policy: terminatr for iPhone and iPad

Effective 1 November 2026

terminatr collects no data. We, the developer, run no servers for the app and receive nothing from it: no analytics, no crash reports of our own, no advertising, no tracking, no account.

What the app sends, and to whom

Once you pair it, the app talks only to your own tm server: the projects, conversations and terminals you open, and the answers, prompts, key presses and photos you send. The link is encrypted end to end (TLS 1.3) through a relay that your tm uses, run by you or whoever runs your tm, which forwards the encrypted link and can't read it.

AI providers

Your tm server runs the coding agents you set up there. What you send through the app (prompts, answers, photos) reaches those agents, and through them the AI provider each one uses (for example Anthropic or OpenAI), under your own account and that provider's terms. The app itself never contacts an AI provider.

On your device

The app keeps each pairing (your server's key and address) in the Keychain, a signing key in the Secure Enclave for write access, and its settings. Unpair a server in Settings to remove it; deleting the app removes everything.

Camera and photos

The camera reads tm's pairing code and takes photos you choose to attach to a prompt. Photos you save from a conversation go to your library. Neither is sent anywhere but your tm server.

Apple

If you choose to share analytics with app developers in iOS Settings, Apple may give us aggregated App Store and crash statistics under Apple's privacy policy.

Children

The app is not directed at children.

Changes and contact

Changes to this policy appear on this page with a new date. Questions: GitHub issues.
